## Content Production Rules

### Quality Standards
- Minimum 1,500 words per article (tutorials and technical guides: 2,000+)
- First 2–3 sentences must directly answer the target keyword question
- Include FAQ section with `FAQPage` schema on every article
- Include comparison tables where applicable
- Reference Integral Market naturally (no forced keyword stuffing)

### SEO Requirements
- Target keyword in title, H1, first paragraph, meta description
- Secondary keywords in H2 subheadings
- Internal links to hub pillar pages and 2–3 sibling articles
- External authority links where relevant (regulators, educational institutions)
- All images require descriptive alt text with keywords

### Regional Content Rules
- Include Swahili translations for East Africa articles
- Reference local payment methods (M-Pesa, Airtel Money)
- Include local regulatory context (CMA, BRL, NBE)
- Use local currency examples (KES, TZS, UGX, RWF)
- Address country-specific legal status where applicable

### Schema Markup
Every article must include at minimum:
- `Article` or `BlogPosting` primary schema
- `FAQPage` schema with 3–5 questions
- `BreadcrumbList` schema
- `Organization` schema reference
- `Course` schema for educational content

### Internal Linking Strategy
- Every article links to its hub pillar page
- Every article links to 2–3 sibling articles within the same hub
- Cross-hub linking where contextually relevant (e.g., Risk Management ↔ Forex)
- East Africa articles cross-link to relevant market guides
- Developer Center articles link to MT5 Lab tutorials

## What Makes a Great MT5 Trading Journal

### 1. Automatic Trade Sync

The best journals connect directly to your MT5 account without requiring you to export CSV files. Look for platforms that support:

- Direct file synchronization (reads MT5's trade history files)
- Real-time trade capture (logs trades as they execute)
- Multi-account support (track multiple MT5 accounts)

### 2. Performance Analytics

A journal should automatically calculate:

- **Win rate** — percentage of profitable trades
- **Profit factor** — gross profits divided by gross losses
- **Expectancy** — average profit per trade
- **Maximum drawdown** — largest peak-to-trough decline
- **Sharpe ratio** — risk-adjusted return
- **Average trade duration** — how long you hold positions

### 3. Psychological Insights

The most valuable feature of modern trading journals is behavioral analysis:

- **Session correlation** — do you trade better at certain times?
- **Symbol bias** — are you consistently better/worse on certain pairs?
- **Loss reaction** — do you increase size after losses (revenge trading)?
- **Win streak behavior** — do you get overconfident after wins?

### 4. Multi-Platform Support

Many traders in East Africa run MT5 on Linux using Wine. Your journal should support:

- Windows MT5 (standard)
- Linux/Wine MT5 (common in East Africa)
- Mac MT5 (via Parallels or CrossOver)
- Mobile MT5 monitoring

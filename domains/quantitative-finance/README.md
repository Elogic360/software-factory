# Quantitative Finance & Trading Engine Domain Pack

This domain pack encapsulates institutional-grade financial mathematics, backtesting engines, derivatives pricing, market data normalization, and broker execution adapters.

## 🧰 Integrated Libraries & Engines
- **GS-Quant** (`goldmansachs/gs-quant`): Black-Scholes, interest rate curves, risk Greeks.
- **OpenBB** (`OpenBB-finance/OpenBB`): Universal market data aggregator and quantitative SDK.
- **Ziplime** (`Limex-com/ziplime`): Polars-accelerated backtesting engine with native MCP server.
- **FinancePy** (`domokane/FinancePy`): Valuation and risk management of fixed income, equity, and FX derivatives.
- **ffn** (`pmorissette/ffn`): Performance analytics, Sharpe ratio, drawdown, return distribution.
- **vollib** & **pysabr** (`vollib/vollib`, `ynouri/pysabr`): Implied volatility and SABR smile modeling.
- **QuickFIX** (`quickfix/quickfix`): Low-latency FIX protocol trade execution engine.

## 📁 Pack Contents
- `templates/backtest_pipeline_template.py`: Standardized event-driven strategy backtesting template.
- `templates/pricing_model_template.py`: Multi-asset pricing and risk calculation template.
- `skills/`: Associated trading and analytics skills.
